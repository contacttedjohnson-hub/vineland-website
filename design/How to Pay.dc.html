How to Pay

Three ways to pay rent — and with us, the price is the price. No taxes, no added fees, no teaser rates that jump after a month. The one exception: card payments carry the card company's processing fee, which is why most folks pay by check, cash, or bank bill pay.

Pay by bank bill pay

Recommended

Nearly every bank — Wells Fargo, Chase, Bank of America, local credit unions — has a free feature on its website and app, usually called Bill Pay. You add us as a payee once, and your bank mails us a check each month. It costs you nothing, there are no processing fees, and it arrives like any other check.

Best of all: set it to repeat automatically and rent handles itself. No stamps, no logins, no remembering.

Set it up in five steps
  1. Log into your bank's website or app and find Bill Pay (sometimes "Pay bills").
  2. Add a new payee: Vineland Development Corp
  3. Mailing address: 1771 Executive Rd, Winter Haven, FL 33884
  4. In the memo or account field, put your name and unit number so we credit the right account.
  5. Schedule the payment about a week before the 1st, and set it to repeat monthly. Done — for good.

Not sure how to find Bill Pay at your bank? Call us — we've walked plenty of folks through it.

Pay online by card

Prefer a card? You can pay online in about a minute — no account or login needed. At checkout you'll enter your name and unit number so we credit the right account, and your receipt comes by email.
