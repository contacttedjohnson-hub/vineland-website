A family business since 1972

Vineland Development has been renting space on Executive Road since 1972 — over fifty years, same family, same phone number habit: when you call, one of us picks up.

What started as a handful of buildings has grown to 134 drive-up units across seven buildings and 75 parking spaces. But it still runs the way a small-town family business should: month-to-month rentals, plain dealing, and tenants who stay for years because it works for them.

Some of our tenants build cabinets. Some run their trade out of a bay. Some just need a dry place for the boat or the extra furniture. Whatever the reason, we'll treat you like a neighbor — because around here, you probably are.
