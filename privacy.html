Privacy Policy

Vineland Development Corp. ("we," "us") rents storage units and parking spaces at 1771 Executive Rd, Winter Haven, FL 33884. We're a family business, and we treat your information the way we'd want ours treated: we collect only what we need to rent you a space and run your account, and we don't sell it to anyone.

What personal information we collect

When you rent a space from us or apply to, we collect your name, mailing address, email address, phone number (including mobile), the unit or parking space you rent, and your payment and account history with us. If you pay online by card, your card details are entered directly with our payment processor (Stripe) on a secure payment page — we never see or store your full card number. Our website itself has no user accounts and does not set advertising or tracking cookies; our hosting provider keeps standard server logs.

How we use personal information

We use your information to manage your rental account: preparing invoices and receipts, taking payments, sending payment reminders and account notices by mail, email, or text message, providing any notices required by Florida law, and responding when you contact us. We do not use your information for third-party marketing, and we do not sell it.

Text messages (SMS)

If you give us your mobile number on your rental agreement, we use it only for messages about your own account — things like payment reminders and account notices. SMS consent is not shared with third parties. No mobile information or text-messaging opt-in data will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. You can stop our texts at any time by replying STOP, or reply HELP for help — full details are in our SMS Terms of Service. Message and data rates may apply; message frequency varies.

Who we share personal information with

We share information only with the service providers who help us run the business — our payment processor (Stripe), the providers that deliver our email and text messages, and our website host — and only what they need to do that job. We may also disclose information when the law requires it (for example, in response to legal process) or to our professional advisors. We never sell your personal information and never share it for anyone else's marketing.

How long we keep it, and how we protect it

We keep account records while you rent from us and afterward as long as tax, accounting, and legal requirements make us. We use reasonable safeguards to protect the information we hold.

Your choices and how to reach us

You can opt out of text messages any time by replying STOP. To ask a question about this policy, or to review or correct your information, call us at 863-324-6591, stop by the office, or write to Vineland Development Corp., 1771 Executive Rd, Winter Haven, FL 33884.

If we change this policy, we'll post the updated version on this page with a new effective date.
