How to Pay

Rent is due on the 1st. Here's how to pay it.

Three ways, and the price is the price. No added fees, no taxes, no rate that jumps after the first month. The one exception is paying by card, which carries the card company's processing fee. That's why most of our tenants pay by check, cash, or bank bill pay.

01 Recommended

Bank bill pay. Free, and it runs itself.

Nearly every bank has a free feature on its website and app, usually called Bill Pay. Wells Fargo, Chase, Bank of America, the local credit unions, all of them. You add us as a payee once and your bank mails us a check each month. It costs you nothing and it arrives like any other check.

Set it to repeat every month and rent handles itself. No stamps, no logins, no remembering.

Set it up once
  1. Log in to your bank's website or app and find Bill Pay. Some banks call it "Pay bills."
  2. Add a new payee: Vineland Development Corp
  3. Payee address: 1771 Executive Rd, Winter Haven, FL 33884
  4. In the memo or account field put your name and unit number so we credit the right account.
  5. Schedule it about a week before the 1st and set it to repeat monthly. That's it.

Can't find Bill Pay at your bank? Call us. We've walked plenty of people through it.

02

Pay online.

Prefer to pay from your phone? You'll be able to pay online in about a minute with no account to set up. Your receipt comes by email.

Before you pay by card Card payments include a 3% processing fee. That's the card company's charge, not ours, and it's shown before you confirm. We take credit cards only; debit and prepaid cards won't go through. Every other way to pay is fee-free.

04

Due dates and late fees.

Rent is due on the 1st of each month. If it hasn't reached us by the 10th, a late fee is added. If something's gone wrong, a check got lost, a payment didn't go through, money's tight this month, call before the 10th. We'd rather hear from you than send a letter.
