Spaces & Parking

Every unit is enclosed, dry, and at ground level — pull your truck right up to the door. What you do inside is up to you: workshop, business storage, household overflow.

Drive-up units

We have 134 units across seven buildings, from 10×10 up to 46×25. They're rented month-to-month, and our tenants put them to all kinds of use:

Workshops Woodworking, fabrication, repair — space to actually work in.
Trades Contractors and tradesmen keeping tools, materials, and equipment close.
Small business Inventory overflow, e-commerce stock, seasonal goods.
Household Plain, dry storage for the things that don't fit at home.
Sizes

Common sizes include 10×10, 10×20, 12×25, 20×25, and larger bays up to 46×25. Not every size is open at any given time — call for current rates and we'll tell you what fits.

Vehicle, boat & RV parking

75 parking spaces for boats, trailers, RVs, and work vehicles — spots from 12×20 up to 12×40.

Open Straightforward open-air spaces — the simple option.
Covered Roofed spaces that keep the Florida sun and rain off your boat or RV.
Overflow Extra room when you need a spot for a season or a project.

Rates and availability

We don't post prices online — rates depend on the unit and what's open. Availability changes often; call and we'll tell you what's open today, or put you on the list for what you need.
