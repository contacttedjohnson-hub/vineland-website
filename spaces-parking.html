Spaces & Parking

Every unit is enclosed, dry, and at ground level.

Pull your truck up to the door. What you do inside is up to you: a project, business storage, or a place for the boat and the extra furniture.

Drive-up units

134 units across seven buildings.

Sizes run from 10×10 up to 46×25, all enclosed, all at ground level. Most units have their own electric meter, so lights and tools run on your own account. Everything is month to month, and most of our tenants have been here for years.

Work garages Space to actually work in, with power in your own name.
Trades Contractors keeping tools, materials, and equipment close and off the truck.
Hobby garages A car project, a bike, a boat motor. Somewhere to spend a Saturday that isn't your driveway.
Storage Inventory overflow, seasonal stock, or plain household storage. Dry and drive-up.

Parking

75 spaces for boats, trailers, RVs, and work vehicles.

Rates and availability

We don't post prices. Call and ask.

Rates depend on the unit and what's open, and that changes week to week. Call, tell us what you need, and we'll tell you what's available and what it costs. If nothing fits today, we'll put you on the waiting list.
