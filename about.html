About

The buildings went up in 1972. The same family has run them since.

Vineland Development is seven buildings on Executive Road in Winter Haven: 134 drive-up units and 75 parking spaces, rented month to month, run out of one small office at 1771 Executive Road with the buildings right behind it.

Our tenants are a mix. Some keep inventory and equipment for a business. Some keep what doesn't fit at home. Some have a garage here where they work on a car on Saturdays or run a small trade, and a few have been in the same units for decades. Most of them have been with us long enough that we know their trucks.

We've never tried to be a national storage chain. There's no call center. When you phone, you reach the office, and if nobody's free you leave a message and we call you back. When something breaks, we come look at it.

How we work

Month to month, with a plain agreement you can read in one sitting. The rate we quote is the rate you pay. Most units are metered, and power goes in your own name through TECO, so your electric bill is yours and nobody else's. If a unit isn't open when you call, we keep a real waiting list and we actually call it.
