About

The buildings went up in 1972. The same family has run them since.

Vineland Development is seven buildings on Executive Road in Winter Haven: 134 drive-up units and 75 parking spaces, rented month to month, run out of one small office.

About six in ten of our units are plain storage. Businesses keep inventory and equipment in them, and families keep the things that don't fit at home. The rest are working garages. Some are weekend places where somebody is rebuilding a car or a boat motor. Some are full-time shops, cabinet makers and repair businesses that have been here for years, a few of them in two or three units with the walls taken out between them.

We've never tried to be a national storage chain. There's no gate code hotline and no call center. When you phone during office hours, one of us picks up. When something breaks, we come look at it. Most of our tenants have been with us long enough that we know their trucks.

How we work

Month to month, with a plain agreement you can read in one sitting. The rate we quote is the rate you pay. Units with a meter get power in your own name through TECO, so your electric bill is yours and nobody else's. If a unit isn't open when you call, we keep a real waiting list and we actually call it.
